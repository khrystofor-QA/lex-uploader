Privacy policy

Lex uploader · last updated 24 November 2026

What the app is

Lex uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel ЕГЭ наглядно. It uploads that owner's own videos to that channel and reads the view counts of those videos. It has no other users.

Data accessed

  • Permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) — used to publish the owner's videos.
  • Read-only access to the channel (youtube.readonly) — used to confirm which channel is connected and to read the view counts of the uploaded videos.

How data is stored and shared

The access token and view counts are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. View counts of public videos may be reported by the owner to the platform that pays for those videos.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted together with the token.
